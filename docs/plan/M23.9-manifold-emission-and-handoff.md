# M23.9 — Manifold emission, aliveness, projector handoff, far stars

Status: implemented as one working-tree change (2026-10-31), not committed. Flat/solid and
surface handoff both shipped; the 2-D *flat* token view keeps its UI-drawn tokens (the
renderer emits no pieces there), so it has footprints/heat but no copies. Spec source:
`visual_ideas.md` "Approved direction" A-F. Follows M23.8. Written before the code, from
reading the real files; the "Findings" corrections below were found by that reading.

## Findings that change the brief

- `backdrop.frag/.vert` and `BoardRenderer::buildBackdropPipeline` exist but **nothing
  calls the builder and nothing draws it**. The room a player sees is the clear colour
  `theme.ink` plus the ImGui `DepthField` grid floor (`drawGridFloor`, `src/render/deco.cpp`)
  submitted through the `background` callback. B therefore *wires the backdrop pass up*
  (create the pipeline, draw it first in `BoardRenderer::record`, before `background`),
  gated on LED-cell themes so other themes' captures do not change.
- The ImGui grid floor cannot see the board's screen position. Ground spill is done in the
  backdrop pass as a floor-region ellipse under the board (the grid lines draw over it),
  not by changing `drawGridFloor`.
- `fragPanelSignal` is `{seed, responseGain, effectWeight}`. Heat/footprint needs its own
  channel: it becomes `vec4` (`.w`), `MeshVertex` 68 -> 72 bytes, `Instance` 96 -> 100.
- The LED "gaps" are not black today (`0.82 + 0.22 * pixels`); the emission pass makes
  light squares' gaps dark and puts the emission in the emitter centres, keeping the mean
  brightness of a far (undersampled) cell roughly unchanged.

## A+B. Emission, scene light, separation, bezel

Files: `board.frag` (matId 7), `backdrop.frag`, `board_renderer.cpp` (backdrop wiring,
bezel), new `src/render/board_glow.{hpp,cpp}`, `src/view/theme.hpp` (manifold hues).

- Emission amount per cell = smoothstep of the cell colour luminance (light squares and
  bright gameplay colours emit; dark squares do not). Emitter profile = existing rect mask
  times a radial bleed (`0.55 + 0.9 * radial`); both go through the existing `fwidth` /
  `undersampled` filter (radial collapses to its mean). The "glow" is an in-shader
  approximation (a faint face lift) plus the backdrop halo; no extra pass, no whole-screen
  bloom. Seams/portal rails are other materials and are untouched.
- `boardGlow(positions, viewProj, boardRect, targetW, targetH)` (pure, tested): projects a
  strided sample of cell centres, returns the screen-space centre/half-size in target uv.
  Backdrop push carries that, the emission tint, camera basis and a ray mapping.
- Hue: manifold `ink/soot` move to neutral near-black with a slight violet; board stays cyan.
- Bezel: for LED themes the plinth's `edge` colour is the board emission colour at higher
  strength (the existing rim band, brighter), never the seam ramp.

## C. Aliveness (`board.frag` + pure mirror `src/render/panel_alive.{hpp,cpp}`)

Per-effect bounds replace the +-0.04 clamp:
breathing +-0.05, refresh sweep +0.06, stuck/flicker emitters (about 1 cell in 40)
+-0.18 on one emitter row, glitch kinds (row shift <= 0.25 of a cell, tear band, channel
offset <= 0.06 of a cell, dropout to 0.15 then settle) one cell at a time. Glitches are
scheduled on a global slot clock (`kGlitchSlot` = 1.7 s): in each slot at most the cells whose
`seed16 % modulus == slotHash % modulus` fire, with `modulus` proportional to the LED cell
count (pushed as `visual.z`), so the expected rate is about 0.25 per slot regardless of board
size and two cells in one slot is a collision case of probability about `1/modulus` each.
Duration 0.08-0.25 s. Cells carrying a cue (selection, legal/peek/last-move/hint colour, check)
get **negative** `effectWeight`: glitches off, breathing kept. All pure in
`push.visual.x`. The C++ mirror `panelGlitch(seed16, seconds, modulus)` is what the tests
check; the GLSL implements the same integer hash by hand and the comment says so.

### C addendum (user spec update, same day)

- **Flicker is first-class.** About one cell in six carries one flickering or stuck emitter
  (14 Hz irregular ticks: dark, normal or a 1.7x spike), so a handful flicker at any moment
  and read at play distance, each confined to one emitter. Calm mode keeps the stuck ones
  static. Glitches: `kGlitchSparsity` 2 (about one per two 1.5 s slots), tear brightens 40 %.
- **Living colour change.** `PanelResponse` already holds `{lastTarget, changedAt}`; it now
  also keeps the colour a change *left* and writes `panelPrev = {from.rgb, age}` into the new
  per-instance / per-vertex `panelPrev` channel (age negative = settled; first exposure,
  rewind and ages past `kTransitionMaxSeconds` = 0.6 s are settled, so a still capture shows
  the final colour). `board.frag` blends `from -> target` per emitter with a seeded
  `emitterTransition(seed, ix, iy)`: 80 % delay <= 40 ms + duration 40-80 ms (done by
  120 ms), 18 % tail ending 0.18-0.48 s, 2 % lazy ending 0.40-0.60 s, plus a 10 % brightness
  overshoot as an emitter lands (scaled by intensity). Undersampled cells use one average
  timing. The 1.8 % `panelSignal[1]` settle stays. Vertex attribute locations 15 (vertex) and
  16 (instance) are added: the pipeline now needs 16 input locations after the second pass packing.
- Tests: `tests/render/test_panel_alive.cpp` (fraction complete at 120 ms in 0.76-0.84, tail
  10-24 %, max end <= 0.6 s, monotone progress), `test_panel_response.cpp` (age/old colour
  hand-off, settled cases).

## D. Projector handoff (`src/view/move_anim.{hpp,cpp}`, `board_renderer.cpp`)

- `view::handoffState(path, progress, intensity)` (pure): positions = `from` then each
  `step.to` (deduped; leap/unexplained/no steps: `{from,to}`; intensity 0 = calm = `{from,to}`
  crossfade with no trail). Hop `j` is centred at `0.85 * j / N`; copy `j` rises over one
  hop, fades over two (the trail), intermediates peak at 0.55 opacity and are scan-dominated
  (`scan` 0.75); only the destination resolves (`scan` -> 0) in the final 15 %.
  Heat for cell `j`: rises from two hops ahead of the leading copy to full one half-hop
  ahead, cools with afterglow, always back to 0 by progress 1 (the destination eases to the
  standing footprint instead).
- `kFootprintHeat` = 0.22: every occupied cell keeps a faint lit base; the caller takes
  `max(footprint, heat)`.
- Rendering: copies are alpha-blended `byShapeFlourish` instances (`panelSignal[3]` on a piece
  = scan dominance, read by the hologram branch), the destination copy goes in the opaque
  batch once opacity reaches 1. The continuous glide `MoveAnimation::sample()` is untouched,
  so `move_camera`/shape-follow still read one continuous virtual position.
  `MoveAnimation::start` caps total duration (`kMaxMoveSeconds` = 1.5 s) by shrinking per-cell
  time and the portal pause together.
- Heat goes into `Instance::panelSignal[3]` (flat) and the sampled vertices (surface), set in
  `buildInstances` from a per-cell map; the heat is a pure function of progress so it needs no
  `PanelResponse` history (the response helper keeps `panelSignal[1]` only).
- Surface: `emitSurfacePiece` copies at the seats of the same cells; the single contact shadow
  pinned to `SurfaceMoveSample::ground` is dropped in favour of the per-hop footprint.

## Second pass (2026-10-31, revised bullets in visual_ideas.md)

- **Capture on LED cells** has no slab: `captureFlash` is skipped when the cell material is
  LED (flat and surface paths). `view::capturePulse(progress, duration)` (pure) drives the
  captured cell's own emitters: from contact (0.55) the cell colour is the capture colour with
  `panelPrev` = its own colour (per-emitter transition in), from release (0.72) the colour is
  its own with `panelPrev` = the capture colour (transition back). Its `PanelTarget` is not
  reported meanwhile, so `PanelResponse` forgets and re-observes it settled. Non-LED keep the slab.
- **Wandering faults** replace fixed flicker emitters: `emitterFault` (mirrored in
  `board.frag`): per cell about 1 in 6, two slots, period 8-60 s (divisors of 120), fault in the
  first 70 % of each period (never permanent), fades in/out, position re-hashed per period;
  blinkers blink 1-3/s (6 ticks/s, a quarter dark). Calm (intensity < 0.5) removes them.
- **Handoff, solid head**: copy `j` rises at `j * hop` within `min(0.05, hop / 4)`, the left copy
  drops to `kHandoffGhostOpacity` (0.40) and decays over two hops; nothing ahead of the head;
  destination solid on landing (`kHandoffSpan` 0.90). Heat-up ahead unchanged.
- Look: light-square emitter gain roughly doubled, bezel adds `edge * 0.55`, heat is a small
  warm-white glow (radius 0.17-0.28 of the cell, squared falloff) under the piece base.
- Vertex inputs packed to 16 locations: `edgeMask`+`roll` share a `vec2` at 7, instance
  `panelPrev` takes location 8, vertex `panelPrev` is 15.
- Known edge: a capture pulse's lazy emitters can be a few frames short on a very short move.

## E. Far stars (`backdrop.frag`)

Fullscreen ray reconstruction from the camera basis; 3-D hashed direction cells; cool
neutral; rotate with orbit, never translate; fade inside the board's silhouette ellipse.
Twinkle amplitude reads the intensity scalar.

## F. One intensity scalar

`BoardOptions::presentationIntensity` (default 1). `push.visual.y` for the board shader,
backdrop push for stars, `handoffState(..., intensity)` for the trail. Calm (0): no
glitches/sweep/twinkle, no trail; static emitters and footprints stay.

## Acceptance / fallback

Verified by `--shot` captures under the scratchpad. If a captured effect proves too strong,
the constants named `k*` at the top of `board.frag`/`panel_alive.cpp`/`move_anim.cpp` are the
tuning points. If the surface-view handoff proves too deep, flat/solid ship and the surface
keeps the old glide (recorded in Status).
