## §1 Readable HUD

### 1.1 Automatic UI scale

Problem: at 1920×1080 with `guiScale 1`, ledger and tray text is about 9 px tall and hard
to read.

- Add `Settings::guiScaleAuto` (bool, default `true`, persisted as `gui_scale_auto`).
- Effective scale is `guiScale × autoFactor` when auto is on, and `guiScale` alone when it
  is off.
- `autoFactor = clamp(round4(framebufferHeight / 720), 1.0, 3.0)`, where `round4` rounds
  to the nearest 0.25. This gives 720p → 1.0, 1080p → 1.5, 1440p → 2.0 and 2160p → 3.0.
- Put the formula in a pure function, `render::autoUiFactor(int framebufferHeight)`, so
  it can be unit-tested.
- Apply it where `resolveUiScale` is computed (`src/gui/main.cpp`, near line 67).
  Re-apply when the framebuffer height changes (window resize or display change). It
  already goes through `Ui::setScale`, which rebuilds fonts. Only rebuild when the
  effective value actually changes.
- **Captures stay deterministic.** `--shot`, `--clip` and `--trailer-clip` default to auto
  **off**. This keeps every existing `gui-*` capture at its old scale. A new flag
  `--ui-auto` turns auto on for a capture. `--ui-scale N` is always exact and disables
  auto.
- Settings screen: the existing interface-scale control becomes a multiplier labelled
  relative to automatic, plus an "Automatic" toggle.
- Console: `set guiScaleAuto false` through the existing `get`/`set` (add the short alias
  the way M23.10 §1 did for `devOverlay`).

Tests:
- `autoUiFactor` at 600, 720, 900, 1080, 1440, 2160 and 4320, with clamping and
  rounding.
- Settings round trip.
- Capture defaults leave auto off; `--ui-auto` turns it on.

### 1.2 Edge-anchored panels

- **Ledger (`##recent`):** anchor it to the right edge with a fixed margin of
  `16 px × scale`. Fixed width: the widest of "000. Qxh8+ Qxh8+" measured in the readout
  font. It is anchored under the status panel, sizes to the rows present, and grows until
  it reaches the dock minus margins, then scrolls (user review clarification 2026-11-05).
  The row count follows the available height rather than a fixed 14. Keep the M23.5 contract:
  measure rows before `Begin`. The newest row is lit and older rows fade, as today. With
  more rows than fit, scroll so the newest is visible.
- **Trays (`##captured`):** anchor them to the left edge, below the variant plate, with
  the same margin. Same width rule: measure the widest tray row. Show "BY WHITE" and
  "BY BLACK" sections at all times once any capture exists. Piece icons are at least
  `20 px × scale`.
- **Status and variant plate:** keep them in the top corners. Give them the same margin
  so all four panels sit on one grid.

### 1.3 Safe area and framing

- Compute a **stage rect**: the viewport minus the space the panels occupy (left tray
  column, right ledger column, top panel band and bottom dock band). Put the computation
  in a pure helper, `render::stageRect(viewport, panelRects)`, and unit-test it.
- **Do not shrink the viewport.** The board still renders full-screen, with stars behind
  the panels.
- Use the stage rect **only for framing**. Pass the stage rect's aspect and its size
  relative to the viewport into `Session` framing: where `frameOn` and `easeFrameOn`
  choose the distance (headroom), the board must fit inside the stage rect rather than
  the whole viewport.
  - Investigate first whether `view::OrbitCamera` supports an off-centre projection
    (lens shift).
  - If it does, centre the board on the stage rect.
  - If it doesn't, keep it centred on the viewport. The panels are roughly symmetric, so
    only the distance needs the stage rect. Record which approach you took.
- Acceptance: at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 2560×1440, at scales 1, 2 and 3 (and auto), on
  standard, torus `--geometry` and cube5, the default camera shows the board with no part
  under a panel. This includes the case that fails today, where the dock overlaps the
  near corner at scale 3. Check it with a pixel test where practical: board-coloured
  pixels inside the panel rects of a capture rendered with panels hidden.

### 1.4 Dock

- Split `##controls` into three visible groups inside one panel: **game actions** (UNDO,
  RESET, and RESIGN/DRAW if present) | **view controls** (3D, SHAPE, CAMERA, INVERT,
  GHOST) | **MENU**, immediately after the final group gap and rule (user review
  clarification 2026-11-05).
- Put a vertical gap of `12 px × scale` and a 1 px accent rule between groups.
- Centre the dock horizontally at the bottom.
- The hint line ("click a piece, then a lit cell…") moves inside the dock, under the
  buttons, in the label font.
- Button ids, enable rules, M23.7 glitch hover and M23.6 keyboard navigation stay
  unchanged. The existing tests for those must pass unmodified.
- Trailer and watch modes keep their own button sets in the same dock style. The trailer
  timeline strip keeps the bottom edge, with the dock above it.

### 1.5 M23.10 §7 leftovers

- **Dock dims with the boot.** Buttons take the HUD level from `Ui::setHudFx` and multiply
  their text and fill alpha by it. While the dock's content level is below 1, the buttons
  are not clickable.
- **Capture lands in its tray.** A newly captured icon scans in bottom to top over
  0.35 s, with a bright band at the reveal edge, the same look as the boot's piece
  reveal. Its age comes from the capture event's visual time: add a capture serial to
  `FxTimeline` the way M23.10 §3 did for boot.
- **`xN` grouping:** add a unit test for five or more captured pieces of one type.
- **Letter spacing:** ImGui has no tracking, so draw header labels glyph by glyph with
  `0.12 em` extra advance. Add a helper, `widgets::trackedText(drawList, pos, text,
  tracking)`, and use it only for panel headers (MOVES, CAPTURED and similar).

### Tests (§1)

- `[hud-chrome]`, extended:
  - the ledger and trays touch their edge margins;
  - the ledger grows with its content up to the band maximum, then scrolls;

## §2 Floor glow without hard edges or banding

Problem: the floor spill under the manifold board shows as a hard-edged dome with visible
banding (`scratchpad/sheet_hud_1.png`, most visible around the torus). In
`src/render/shaders/backdrop.frag`, line 74 limits the spill with
`smoothstep(0.40, 0.52, uv.y)`, which gives a narrow horizontal edge. The exponential
falloffs then quantise to 8-bit steps.

- **Reproduce it first.** Capture the torus `--geometry` in manifold with `--post 0` and
  `--post 1`. Measure the step: take the pixel luminance profile along a vertical line
  through the spill and record the largest jump between neighbouring pixels. Then fix
  the cause in each path.
- **Shape:**
  - Replace the horizon `smoothstep` with a wide, soft ramp: `smoothstep(0.25, 0.60,
    uv.y)` or a Gaussian in `uv.y`, whichever has no visible edge.
  - Make the spill an ellipse in floor space: project the board centre onto the floor
    plane and take the distance in the camera's ground plane rather than in screen uv,
    so it stays elliptical as the camera orbits. If that is too invasive, keep screen
    space but soften it.
  - No term may have a first-derivative discontinuity anywhere in the frame.
- **Banding:**
  - With post on, the composite's TPDF dither should cover the backdrop. Check that the
    backdrop is actually rendered into the HDR scene before the dither, and fix it if it
    isn't.
  - With post off (the legacy path), add a ±1 LSB TPDF dither in `backdrop.frag` itself,
    using the same hash as `post_composite.frag` (share the GLSL through the existing
    include mechanism, or copy it with a comment pointing at the source).
- **Acceptance:** along the measured profile, no step between neighbouring pixels is
  larger than 2/255 in the smooth region, with post on and with post off. No visible edge
  in captures of standard, torus and klein at several orbit angles (`--script` orbit or
  the camera flags).

Tests:
- A GPU profile test as described in Acceptance.
- The existing backdrop and ground tests still pass. Pin them to `--post 0` only if they
  assert the old shape.

## §3 Result panel at game end

### Behaviour

When the M23.10 §5 end sequence reaches its hold (`kEndDelaySeconds` + wave + fall; use
the same age function), a centred **result panel** powers on:
- It uses the `hudPanel` language with edges tracing over 0.4 s, then content fading in.
- Large title: **CHECKMATE**, **STALEMATE**, **DRAW**, **RESIGNATION** or
  **TIME/RULE** names. Take the reason from `Game`'s end-reason enum and the
  variant-specific outcome text already used by `statusLine`.
- Subtitle: **WHITE WINS**, **BLACK WINS** or **DRAWN**, in the winning side's colour.
  Use neutral for a draw.
- Detail line: the move count ("in 23 moves"), plus the draw rule when there is one
  (repetition, 50-move and so on).
- Buttons: **REMATCH** (the Reset action, which boots, M23.10 §3), **REVIEW** (below) and
  **MENU** (to the main menu through the normal path).
- The panel never covers the mated king's cell at the default camera. Place it in the
  stage rect (§1.3), offset away from the king's projected position. Pure helper:
  `render::resultPanelPos(stage, kingScreenPos, panelSize)`.
- **Dismissal:** an × button or Esc collapses the panel to a small "RESULT" tab in the
  status panel, and clicking the tab reopens it. Undo out of the finished game removes
  the panel along with the power-up.
- **Calm intensity:** no trace animation, just a fade.

### Review

A non-destructive history browser over the finished game:
- `Session` gains a review cursor: `std::optional<std::size_t> reviewPly`. When it is set,
  `snapshot()` shows the position after `reviewPly` moves, rebuilt by replaying from the
  start the same way `capturedBySide` does. Game state is not modified, and
  `game().position()` stays final.
- New `ActionKind`s:
  - `ReviewStart`, `ReviewEnd`;
  - `ReviewStep` (`dx` = ±1);
  - `ReviewJump` (`text` = ply).
- While reviewing:
  - board clicks only select or highlight; they never move;
  - the last-move highlight shows the move at the cursor;
  - the ledger row at the cursor is lit;
  - clicking a ledger row jumps to it.
- Keys: ← and → step, Home and End jump, Esc leaves review (cursor cleared, final
  position). The dock shows ◀ ▶ and EXIT REVIEW in place of the game actions while
  reviewing. Keep M23.6 key-ownership rules.
- The board stays at the end-hold power level during review: it is a dimmed post-game
  board. The move animation plays for single steps forward. Jumps are instant.
- A FEN-started game with no recorded start can't be replayed. Hide REVIEW for it, and
  record this in the same way as the `capturedBySide` guard.
- If review needs more than about 300 changed lines, it may be split into its own commit
  (§3b) after Rematch and Menu ship.

### Access

- Console:
  - `result` shows the panel now, for the current decided game, skipping the delay;
  - `review [<ply>|off]`.
- CLI:
  - `--result` with `--end-t` captures the panel;
  - `--review <ply>` captures a review frame.

### Tests

- `resultPanelPos` keeps clear of the king and stays inside the stage.
- Title and subtitle mapping for every end reason, including a variant with no royal
  piece.
- Session review:
  - the snapshot at ply k equals a fresh game with k moves played (hash compare);
  - review never changes `game()`;
  - clicks don't move while reviewing;
  - ledger jump;
  - exit returns to final;
  - FEN-started games refuse review.
- UI: REMATCH resets and boots; MENU leaves; Esc collapses to the tab; undo removes the
  panel.
- Captures: Fool's mate at `--end-t` 9 and 12 with `--result`, a stalemate, and review at
  ply 2. Manifold and wood.

## §4 Live HUD feedback

All of these are pure in visual time, driven by `FxTimeline` serials (add any missing
serials: turn change, engine start and stop, new move). All scale with
`presentationIntensity`; at 0 the state still changes, but instantly.

- **Turn flip:** the status panel's turn LED and "WHITE TO MOVE" text cross-fade to the
  other side over 0.25 s, with one LED flicker at the switch.
- **Engine thinking:** while `Session::engineThinking()`, a three-dot LED sequence pulses
  after the turn text (each dot 0.4 s, staggered by 0.13 s), and "THINKING" appears in
  the label font. It stops the frame the move is chosen.
- **Check:** "CHECK" appears in the status panel in the check colour, with a 0.3 s
  brightness spike timed to the §4 ripple's start (same event). It persists while in
  check. The power-down result text replaces it at the end hold.
- **New move in the ledger:** the new row's text reveals left to right over 0.2 s, with a
  bright leading edge, the "living transition" in text form. Older rows shift up with a
  0.15 s ease rather than jumping.

Tests: a pure function for each timing; a capture at mid-transition for each (use
`--script` plus the event-time flags; add `--event-t <s>` if no existing flag can pin the
age); `presentationIntensity 0` gives an instant state change.

second clock.

  - the menu panels trace *off* (the reverse of the panel trace) over 0.3 s;
