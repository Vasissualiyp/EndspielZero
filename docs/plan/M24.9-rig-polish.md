## 1 — The shape sits on its slab

In the shape view, a 2-D shape (torus, cylinder, Möbius, Klein) sits diagonally off its
slab even when flat, so the spokes to the yoke are long (M24.5b Status). Centre the
formed and the flat shape on the slab's carrier.
- **Test:** the shape's bounding-box centre is within 0.05 m of the yoke axis at morph 0
  and at morph 1, and the spokes are ≤ 0.35 m long.

## 2 — Ribs you can see

- **Closed torus:** the ribs are inside the tube, so they are only visible mid-morph. Make
  the structure read on the formed torus from the default game camera. Pick the smaller
  change that looks right, and record it:
  - thin hoops on the outside between tile rows; or
  - a membrane that lets the ribs show faintly.
- **Klein:** short rib pieces stand outside the skin where the playing side faces into
  the bottle. Remove any rib piece that is not covered by the skin from the camera's
  side, or route it inside.
- **Möbius:** check that the ribs stop cleanly at the twisted seam (M24.5b deviation 4).
  There must be no floating stubs.

## 3 — The game camera frames the board

With M24.5's default lab game camera, the board takes a small part of the screen.
- **Test:** for standard, cylinder, torus, cube5 and hyper4, the board's projected hull
  is ≥ 22 % of the frame height and ≥ 10 % of the frame area. All pieces stay
  on-screen, and the HUD panels cover ≤ 10 % of the board's hull.
- Keep the rig visible: at least the mast and the slab edge.

## 4 — Stow clears the slab

During a stow, about 1.5 s in, the camera passes just under the carried slab (M24.5
deviation 2).
- **Test:** across the whole deploy and stow at 60 Hz, the eye keeps ≥ 0.30 m from the
  carried slab's box, and never passes between the slab and the gantry rail.

## 5 — The Watch station looks at something

The M24.4e Watch station is physical, but its view is mostly the side of the rack and
dark space, with the bench far off. Re-pick it (standing rule unchanged) so that the
view holds the bench projector and its hologram (where the replay is shown) or the
deployed board's spot. Keep the Watch panel on the right, not over the subject.
- **Test:** the bench projector or the hologram is ≥ 3 % of the frame, inside the left
  60 % of the width. The rack's side panel is ≤ 15 %.

## 6 — The Library panel is not over the rack

In the M24.4e Library view, the rack sits right behind the menu panel, which looks
busy. If you can, keep the standing rule and M24.4e's relaxed numbers and get the rack's
on-screen box to overlap the panel rect by ≤ 30 % of the rack box. If you cannot, record
the best you found and leave it.
