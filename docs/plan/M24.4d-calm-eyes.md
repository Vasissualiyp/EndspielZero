# M24.4d — Calm eyes: a still camera, and daydreaming instead of wandering

Status: **planned 2026-11-13** from play-testing M24.4c/M24.7. Implementer: Claude Sonnet.
Requires M24.4c and M24.7 (committed).

Read first: `docs/plan/M24.4c-living-vision.md` §1.5 (gaze state machine), §4.5 (eye
shift on the camera), §4.6; `docs/plan/M24-bci-look.md`. This slice overrides them where
they disagree.

Play-test feedback this answers:
- "When there's a refocusing, the camera moves a little bit, by a fraction of a degree.
  Should stay in the same place."
- "The focus shift is too frequent and disorienting. Maybe after ~10 s of inactivity,
  apply blur to the whole viewport, and make up random eye movements with areas of
  smaller blur (focus areas)."

## Step 1 — the camera never moves with the gaze

- Remove §4.5: the gaze no longer adds `eyeShift` to the camera's yaw/pitch. Delete
  `eyeShift`, `eyeShiftTarget`, `eyeShiftFrom`, `eyeShiftToward`, `kEyeShiftFraction`,
  `kEyeShiftMaxRadians` and `kDriftRadians` (or whatever M24.4c named them) and their
  tests, unless something else needs them. The saccadic dip keeps its mouse-driven input
  and loses the eye-shift term.
- Grep for anything else that nudges the lab camera while a station is settled (gaze,
  tremor, focus, breathing). The settled camera must be exactly still. Tremor stays on
  the implant glow only, as the brief says.
- **Test:** at a settled station with wandering forced on, the camera's `viewProj` is
  bit-identical across 30 s of 60 Hz gaze steps, through several saccades and focus
  changes.

## Step 2 — no automatic gaze while the player is active

- `kIdleBeforeWander`: 2.5 s → **10 s**.
- While `idle < 10 s`, the gaze follows the mouse only (M24.4c §1.5 item 3), with no
  automatic saccades.

## Step 3 — daydreaming after 10 s idle

This replaces the wandering behaviour of §1.5 item 4. Once `idle ≥ 10 s` (with
`wander` on and intensity ≥ 0.5):

1. **Defocus.** A `daydream` amount eases 0 → 1 over 1.5 s (smoothstep) and blurs the
   **whole viewport**, room and implant panel alike. At `daydream = 1` the room's
   circle of confusion is at least 6 px (1080p), and the panel reads as soft but still
   legible as rows.
   - The room uses the existing depth-of-field pass, with a uniform floor added to the
     circle of confusion.
   - The panel mixes towards `uiSoft` by the same amount.
2. **Focus spots.** A soft-edged disc around the gaze point `pointPx` keeps a smaller
   blur. Inside it the blur falls to 25 % of the daydream blur. It has radius 0.12 H and
   blends over a further 0.06 H. It moves with the existing saccade profile.
3. **Made-up eye movements.** Fixations last **2–5 s**, chosen by the existing seeded
   hash. Each fixation picks one of these:
   - a gaze target (weighted by `weight`, never the same twice in a row, as now);
   - with probability 0.35, a random point inside the middle 80 % of the viewport.

   The room's focus distance follows the fixated target, as now. A random point keeps
   the previous distance.
4. **Waking.** Any mouse motion, click or key press clears it:
   - `daydream` eases back to 0 within **0.3 s**;
   - the focus spot jumps to the mouse;
   - `idle` resets.
5. Calm intensity, `wander off` and captures without `--gaze-wander 1` never daydream.

Expose `daydream`, the spot centre and the spot radius in whatever uniform block the
post and implant passes already share. Never cover the panel with more than the soft
blur: no veil and no dimming on top of what M24.4c already draws.

**Tests:**
- with input every 5 s, there is no saccade and `daydream` stays 0 for 60 s;
- after 10 s idle, `daydream` reaches 1 within 1.6 s;
- every fixation lasts 2–5 s;
- over 60 s, fixations land on ≥ 3 distinct places;
- input clears `daydream` to < 0.05 within 0.3 s;
- determinism: same seed and inputs give the same states.

A GPU or capture check confirms both of these:
- at `daydream = 1`, a region far from the spot has less high-frequency energy than at
  `daydream = 0`;
- inside the spot it has more than outside.

Console/CLI: keep `wander on|off`, `--gaze-wander`. Add `--daydream-t <s>` for captures
(pins the idle time and the fixation), and list it in `help()`, `docs/debug-console.md`
and the AGENTS.md table.

## Step 4 — sheets and commit

Put the sheets in `out/m24.4d/`:
- `still.png`: two settled frames 3 s apart with wander on, plus their difference image.
  The difference must be zero outside the implant glow.
- `daydream.png`: idle 9, 10, 10.5, 11, 13, 16 and 20 s, then 0.3 s after a mouse move.

Look at them. The daydream should feel like eyes drifting while lost in thought, not
like a camera effect.

Gates as always. Commit: `ui: calm eyes - a still camera and daydreaming after 10 s idle
(M24.4d)`, plus a Status entry.

## Status

**2026-11-13 — implemented.** The camera no longer follows the gaze: `eyeShift*`,
`eyeShiftToward`, `kEyeShiftFraction`, `kEyeShiftMaxRadians` and `kDriftRadians` are gone,
and `stepLiving` leaves `camera` alone. `kIdleBeforeWander` is 10 s and fixations last 2-5 s.
`GazeState::daydream` eases in over 1.5 s (smoothstep on `daydreamT`) and out in 0.3 s on any
mouse motion, drag, click or key (`GazeInput::input`, set from the SDL loop). A fixation is a
weighted target, or with probability 0.35 (or when no target is visible) a seeded point in the
middle 80 % that keeps the previous focus distance. The room blur is `DepthOfField::floorPx`
(6 px at 1080p) with the spot (0.12 H + 0.06 H feather, 25 %) in `dof.frag`
(push block now 64 bytes); the panel mixes towards `uiSoft` by 0.8 x daydream in `implant.frag`
through `ImplantParams::daydream`. `view::daydreamBlurPixels` mirrors the shader and is tested.
Tests: 648 cases pass across [view][render][app]; `ctest -L gui` 54/54; precommit OK.
Sheets: `out/m24.4d/still.png` (two frames 3 s apart, pixel difference 0) and `daydream.png`.
Measured: far-region Laplacian energy falls from 2914 to 357 (daydream 0 -> 1); the focus
spot on the menu holds 197 against 14 outside it.

Deviations: (1) `--daydream-wake <s>` was added beside `--daydream-t` to capture the 0.3 s after
a mouse move; `--daydream-t` replays the idle seconds from a fresh eye each frame. (2) The
"viewProj bit-identical through stepLiving" test is at the `stepGaze` level (stepLiving lives in
`gui/main.cpp`); the guarantee is structural, as the gaze state holds no camera term. (3) The
gaze's own saccadic dip is now 0: the eye-shift speed was its only input. (4) `still.png` pins
`--gaze-target menu` so the spot and focus are the same in both frames; unpinned frames differ
only where the spot moves.
