## What this is

Starting the app is the player opening their eyes in their lab, after which the implant
comes online. In about 5 s, skippable:
1. closed eyes (a faint warm glow through the lids);
2. the lids open with one hesitation, onto a blurred, dim view;
3. two blinks, each sharpening the view;
4. the room is already lit, and the bench projector powers on;
5. the implant handshake: phosphenes, a log-polar ring wave, and colour separation
   converging onto the main menu.

**Every launch** plays it once (the design says first launch: "each launch plays it
once; no other screen change does"). The handshake is the long form only here; every
other panel change keeps M24.4b's short form. Setting `wakeOnLaunch` (default true)
turns it off.

**Where:** lab on and post (HDR) on. With either off, launch does exactly what it does
today. Captures default to **no wake** unless `--wake-t` is given.

## Step 1 — pure timing: `src/view/wake.{hpp,cpp}` (test first)

```cpp
namespace cb::view {
inline constexpr float kWakeSeconds = 5.0f;
struct WakeState {
  float lidOpen{1};        ///< 0 closed .. 1 fully open (both lids)
  float topEdge{0};        ///< fraction of screen height covered by the upper lid at centre
  float bottomEdge{0};     ///< fraction covered by the lower lid at centre
  float curve{0};          ///< how much the lid edges bow (corners open last), 0..1
  float feather{0.04f};    ///< edge softness, fraction of screen height
  float blurFraction{0};   ///< defocus radius as a fraction of screen height
  float exposureEv{0};     ///< 0 normal, negative darker
  float saturation{1};
  float lidGlow{0};        ///< warm glow through closed lids, 0..0.03
  float roomUp{1};         ///< 0..1: projector power-on progress (step 4)
  float handshake{-1};     ///< seconds into the handshake, or -1 before it starts
  bool done{true};
};
[[nodiscard]] WakeState wakeState(float age, float intensity) noexcept;
}
```

The timeline follows the overview exactly. Encode these keyframes:

| Age (s) | Lids | Blur (fraction of H) | Exposure | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 – 0.6 | closed (`lidOpen` 0) | 0.025 | −1.5 | `lidGlow` 0.03 × (0.8 + 0.2 sin(2.1·age)) |
| 0.6 – 1.6 | open: the upper lid moves 1.35× faster than the lower; hesitate at 40 % for 0.15 s (inside this span) | 0.025 → 0.018 | −1.5 → −0.8 | glow fades by 0.8 |
| 1.9 | blink 1: close 0.18 s, open 0.22 s; they meet at 0.47 H (slightly below centre) | 0.018 → 0.010 after it | −0.8 → −0.4 | |
| 2.6 | blink 2: close 0.12 s, open 0.16 s | 0.010 → 0 after it | −0.4 → 0 | saturation 0.7 → 1 by 2.9 |
| 2.0 – 3.2 | open | — | — | `roomUp` 0 → 1 (the projector powers on) |
| 3.0 – 4.8 | open | 0 | 0 | `handshake` = age − 3.0 |
| ≥ 5.0 | `done` | | | |

- **Lid shape:** both edges are curved: the opening is elliptical and wider in the
  middle. `curve` is 1 while opening and blinking, and eases to 0 as the lids fully open.
- **Calm intensity:** no blinks and no hesitation. The lids open once over 0.6 s while the
  blur clears (from 0.025 to 0 over 0.8 s); no handshake (`handshake` stays −1); the
  menu cross-fades in at 0.8 s; `done` at 1.2 s.
- **Tests** (`tests/unit/view/test_wake.cpp`, `[unit][view][wake]`):
  - `lidOpen == 0` for age < 0.6;
  - the hesitation: `lidOpen` is constant (± 0.01) over a 0.12 s window inside 0.6–1.6;
  - at each blink's midpoint, `lidOpen < 0.02`;
  - the blur is non-increasing outside the blinks and 0 after 2.9 s;
  - `done` at 5.0, and at 1.2 for calm;
  - the handshake starts at 3.0;
  - continuity: no field jumps more than its own range / 10 in a 1 ms step, except at
    the documented instants.

## Step 2 — the lids and defocus in the post composite

The eyelids cover the world but **not** the implant layer (the implant is in the
brain): draw them in `post_composite.frag`, which runs before the implant composite.

1. Extend `PostPush` beyond 32 bytes, or move it to a small uniform buffer if push
   constants get tight. Add `lidTop`, `lidBottom`, `curve`, `feather`, `blurPx`,
   `exposureEv`, `saturation`, `lidGlow` and `aspect`. Update the `static_assert`.
2. In the shader, before tone mapping:
   - **defocus:** if `blurPx > 0.5`, replace the scene sample with a 24-tap golden-angle
     spiral (Vogel disc) of radius `blurPx`, equal weights, plus the centre. Exactly
     `texture(scene, uv)` when `blurPx ≤ 0.5`;
   - **exposure:** `rgb *= exp2(exposureEv)`; **saturation:**
     `rgb = mix(vec3(luma), rgb, saturation)`.
3. After tone mapping, grain and dither, apply the **lids**:
   - `y` from 0 at the top to 1 at the bottom; `x` from −1 to 1;
   - the edges at column x: `top(x) = lidTop + curve·0.10·x²` and
     `bottom(x) = 1 − lidBottom − curve·0.10·x²`; the corners close first;
   - `open = smoothstep(top − feather, top + feather, y) ·
     (1 − smoothstep(bottom − feather, bottom + feather, y))`;
   - `outColor.rgb = rgb·open + lidGlowColor·lidGlow·(1 − open)`, where `lidGlowColor`
     is a warm red `#7A2A18` in linear. **When `lidGlow` is 0, the lid region is exactly
     0** (pure black), so a capture mid-blink has rows of zero pixels: test that;
   - **exactness:** with the wake done (all defaults), the shader output equals today's
     byte for byte. Branch on a `wakeActive` flag so the settled path is today's code.
4. `lidTop` and `lidBottom` come from `WakeState`. Map `lidOpen` and the 1.35× upper-lid
   speed into the two coverages, so that at `lidOpen = 0` they meet at 0.47 H.

## Step 3 — the room and the handshake

1. **The room is already lit** (the player's own lab). The bench projector powers on
   with `roomUp`: the M24.3 hologram grows out of the lens using M24.4a's grow, keyed to
   `roomUp`. A short lens flare at `roomUp` 0.05–0.25 comes from a glow-material disc at
   the lens, scaled 0 → 3 → 1 × the lens radius, alpha 1 → 0.
2. **The handshake** uses `ImplantParams::globalSync`, which M24.4b left at 1. Add
   `ringAge` (seconds, −1 = none) and `phosphene` (0..1) to `ImplantParams`, and in
   `implant.frag`:
   - **phosphenes** (handshake 0–0.4 s): 5–8 soft points within 0.25 H of the centre,
     hashed from a launch seed, each appearing and fading over 0.15–0.3 s;
     `implantBody` hue, slightly chromatic (R/B offset 1.5 px). They are drawn even where
     the UI layer is empty: add them to `col` and `a`;
   - **ring wave** (0.2–1.1 s): rings in log-polar space. `rho = log(1 + r / (0.02·H))`;
     the front is at `rho_f = 3.2 · (ringAge / 0.9)`; brightness modulation
     `0.06 · exp(−((rho − rho_f)·4)²) · (0.5 + 0.5 cos(rho·18))`, applied to **both** the
     room (as alpha with an `implantBody` tint, i.e. a faint light over everything) and
     the UI layer. Log spacing makes the rings tight at the centre and wide outside:
     check that by eye;
   - **convergence** (0.4–1.8 s): `globalSync` rises 0 → 1 with smoothstep. M24.4b's
     per-pixel "unsync" becomes `max(panelUnsync, 1 − globalSync)` for every pixel, so
     the colour separation, dissolve and blur apply to the whole layer and resolve onto
     the menu. M24.4b already wrote that path for panels; widen it to the global value;
   - at `globalSync = 1`, `ringAge = −1` and `phosphene = 0`, the output is exactly
     M24.4b/M24.4c's.
3. The main menu pane exists from handshake 0 but is only visible through the
   convergence: its alpha is the global sync.

## Step 4 — wiring, input, settings

1. `main.cpp` keeps `launchSeconds` (visual time at the first frame on the lab path). Each
   frame while not done: compute `WakeState`; feed Step 2's push values and Step 3's
   params; block **menu input** until `handshake ≥ 1.0` (or done).
2. **Skip:** any key or click jumps to done. The menu appears at once, with M24.4b's short
   form.
3. **Replace the current menu fade-in at launch** on the lab path (grep the first-frame
   menu fade: the Ui's initial `enter_` or the main menu's intro fade). Keep it on the
   non-lab path.
4. Setting `wakeOnLaunch` (bool, default true; file key `wake_on_launch`). CLI:
   `--wake-t <s>` pins the age (captures); `--wake 0|1`. Console `wake` replays it
   (re-arms `launchSeconds`), and `handshake` replays only the handshake. Update
   `help()` and `docs/debug-console.md`.

## Step 5 — tests, look, commit

1. The pure tests (Step 1).
2. **GPU:**

     code (exactness);
   - at `--wake-t 1.98` (blink 1 closed), the rows within 0.4 H … 0.55 H are all zero;
