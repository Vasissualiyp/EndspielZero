# M24.4e — Standing stations: the player stands on the floor, and looks down at boards

Status: **planned 2026-11-13** from play-testing. Implementer: Claude Opus. Branch
`m24-rig`. Requires M24.4c-3d-fix (committed).

Read first:
- `docs/plan/M24-the-rig.md`, "Rules for every M24 slice";
- `docs/plan/M24.4c-3d-fix.md`, with its Status;
- the station and flight code and its tests (grep `station`, `RigMove`, `lab.toml`
  `[[station]]`).

The play-test feedback this answers (tested on main, which does not have
M24.4c-3d-fix yet):
- "Watch a game" moves the camera to the old position above the shelves, which is
  unphysical.
- The "select game mode" screen (the Library, the first menu button) puts the player
  above the table, which is unphysical: they should stand in front of the table.
- At the Library the chess board is at eye level, so it is seen slightly from below and
  you cannot read what is happening on it. It should be seen slightly from above. The
  board need not be level with the ground.

## Step 1 — a physical-eye rule for every station

For **every** lab station (menu, tutorial, library, watch, settings, quit, game, paused,
and any others in `lab.toml`):
- the eye's floor point lies **outside every furniture footprint** (desk, shelves, rack,
  lamp base, anything with authored OBJ bounds that stands on the floor), with **≥ 0.25 m**
  clearance, and inside the room walls by ≥ 0.3 m;
- eye height **1.45–1.75 m** (a standing adult), unless the spec of that station already
  says the player sits. Then use 1.10–1.30 m and a chair-sized clear area.

Write this as one test over all stations, using the authored OBJ bounds as M24.4c-3d did.
The menu ↔ station flights keep clearing geometry (the existing clearance test). A
flight's mid-path may rise, but never above 1.9 m.

## Step 2 — fix the stations that break it

- **Watch:** re-pick it so the player stands in front of whatever the watch screen
  shows (the monitor or the board), not above the shelves. Keep what it frames.
- **Library:** the player stands **in front of the table**, on the floor, and looks at
  the wall hologram and the rack. Keep M24.4c-3d-fix's framing numbers (amended:
  hologram ≥ 9 % and ≥ 22 % of the height; rack 7–25 %; x ∈ [0.02, 0.56] W; lamp ≤ 1 %;
  rail ≤ 8 %; pitch ±12°) where they still fit with the new eye.
  - If a number cannot be met from a standing eye, the standing rule wins. Relax the
    number the least you can and record it in the Status entry.
- Any other station the Step 1 test catches: the same treatment.

## Step 3 — the board is seen from above

At the Library, flat subjects (standard, cylinder unrolled, and any 2-D board):
- the line of sight from the eye to the board's centre meets the board's plane at
  **20–40°**, from the board's **top** side, so the pieces stand up towards the viewer;
- the board's centre sits **at or below** eye height, by at least
  0.15 × the board's projected height.

Tilting the board away from horizontal is fine, and so is lowering the projection.
Stacked and 3-D subjects (cube5, hyper4, torus) get the same rule: their top layer is
seen from above, at ≥ 10°.

Add tests computed from the hologram's placement transform and the station eye.

## Step 4 — sheets and commit

Put the sheets in `out/m24.4e/`:
- `stations.png`: every station from its own eye, plus a top-down plan showing each eye
  dot against the furniture footprints;
- `library.png`: the five variants;
- `watch.png`.

Look at them: each should read as a person standing in the room.

Gates as always. Commit: `render: standing stations - the player stands on the floor and
looks down at the board (M24.4e)`, plus a Status entry.
