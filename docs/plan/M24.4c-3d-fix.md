# M24.4c-3d-fix — the Library reads as "looking into the rack, the variant shown big"

Status: **implemented 2026-11-13** (see the last entry below). Implementer: Codex **gpt-6-astra** (3-D framing).
Requires M24.4c-3d (536c1ee). Small slice; do it before M24.5.

Read first: `docs/plan/M24.4c-living-vision.md` Step 0b and its Status (the station
numbers as built), and look at `out/m24.4c/library.png`, `library-flight.png`,
`wall-projector-closeup.png` (regenerate them if missing).

## What is wrong (review of the M24.4c-3d sheets)

The user asked: "look INTO the shelves with railings, and have a bigger projector on the
wall, displaying the variant larger". As built:
1. **The wall hologram is small and edge-on.** Standard is a thin sliver seen from the
   side; hyper4 is a few pixels tall. The Step 0b "≥ 15 %" test measures the sphere's
   bounding cube, not what the eye sees.
2. **The desk lamp's arm dominates the foreground**, crossing between the camera and the
   rack from the bottom-left.
3. The Library pane covers the right ~half of the frame, so rack and hologram compete
   for the left half.

## Changes

1. **Subject size, measured honestly.** Replace the bbox test with one on the
   **projected subject**: render the Library station with `--lab 1` and count the
   pixels the wall hologram's instances cover (the existing test harness can render
   instance IDs or a mask; otherwise compute the screen-space convex hull of the
   subject's instance boxes). Requirement: for standard, cylinder, torus, cube5 and
   hyper4, the subject covers **≥ 9 %** of the frame and its projected height is
   **≥ 22 %** of the frame height.
2. **Tilt the subject towards the viewer.** Flat 2-D subjects (standard, cylinder unrolled
   views) are presented tilted so the camera sees the board's face at **25–40°** above
   edge-on (rotate the subject about its own horizontal axis, keeping it upright for
   stacked/3-D subjects). Raise `kWallHologramRadiusMetres` if needed to meet 1.
3. **Camera.** Re-pick the Library station so that:
   - no lamp part (`lamp.*`) covers more than **1 %** of the frame (move or rotate the
     camera; do not delete the lamp or move it in other stations);
   - the rack's slab bays are seen **into** (the slab faces visible as a row of edges
     receding, not the rack's side panel), the rack covering **7–25 %** of the frame (amended 2026-11-13: 10 % was unreachable with the rack beside the projector);
   - the hologram and the rack both lie in x ∈ **[0.02, 0.56] W** at 16:9 (left of the pane; amended 2026-11-13 from [0.03, 0.52]),
     with the hologram the larger of the two on screen;
   - pitch within ±12°, and the rail/ceiling at most 8 % of the frame;
   - eye height 1.3–1.6 m (amended 2026-11-13 from 1.5 m, wherever an existing test pins it).
   Add tests for each against `OrbitCamera::viewProj` and the authored OBJ bounds, as
   M24.4c-3d did.
4. **Flight.** The menu ↔ library flight (`library-flight.png`) still clears geometry
   (the existing clearance test). If a flight to or from the new station pokes past the room limit, lower the mid-flight lift or route the flight; do not relax the room limit.
5. **Sheets** in `out/m24.4c-fix/`: `library.png` (the five variants), `library-flight.png`,
   `before-after.png` (standard, old vs new). Look at them: the Library must read at a
   glance as "looking into a rack of slabs, with the selected variant projected big on
   the wall".
6. Commit: `render: Library framing - the variant big on the wall, looking into the rack
   (M24.4c-3d-fix)`, plus a Status entry in this file with the final station numbers and
   measured coverages.

## Implementation notes

- **2026-11-13 — investigation / test-first.** The new projected-instance hull
  regression fails 16 of 40 assertions on the original implementation. The old
  sphere test missed actual subject coverages of about 2.10% for torus and 0.43%
  for hyper4, and the rack covers 7.71%. `overtureHologram` uses `scene.cam.reach`,
  which is the old 2-D pane's camera padding, not the subject's geometric radius.
  The Astra design review recommends a wall-only fit to visible scene geometry,
  retaining the bench path, plus a rigid tilt in the existing `Mapping::direction`
  transform. Tilt fades continuously as the subject gains vertical extent, keeping
  formed stacked shapes upright. This wall-only normalization is an addition to
  the spec's radius tuning, needed to remove differing empty padding per variant.
  Tests use the permitted convex-hull fallback: transformed instance boxes, actual
  token-mesh bounds, and viewport-clipped authored hardware bounds. No sphere is
  counted as subject coverage.

- **2026-11-13 — resumption / acceptance corrections.** Rebuilt the interrupted
  tree and reproduced framing and flight failures (31 assertions in 19 focused
  cases). The flat-face angle is measured between the subject normal and the
  subject-to-eye vector, as the spec requires, rather than against world X.
  The old optical-axis yaw limit is superseded by this slice's framing rules;
  the independent slab-front facing check remains. Radius request tests now use
  the named wall-radius constant, so they continue testing interpolation and
  projector choice while the framing regression pins the resulting picture.

- **2026-11-13 — blocked: the station constraints are jointly infeasible as
  measured.** A temporary optimiser (since deleted) scored candidate stations on every
  constraint at once: eye z 1.3–1.5 m, pitch ±12°, rack front within 50° of the eye,
  the M24.4a lens/front anchor window, every eye inside the room for all flights to
  and from the Library (including the `kRigLift` arc), lamp parts ≤ 1 %, ceiling and
  rails ≤ 8 %, rack hull and box 10–25 % in x ∈ [0.03, 0.52], the five subject hulls
  ≥ 9 % / ≥ 22 % tall in the same band and larger than the rack, and the 25–40°
  face angle. Free variables were eye, heading, pitch, fov, distance and
  `kWallHologramRadiusMetres` (0.28–0.45). About 30 hill-climb restarts never found a
  feasible station. The best ones need the eye against the back wall (y ≈ 2.95 m,
  so flight clearance is about 0). The ceiling sits at 8 %, the torus's left edge at
  x = 0.03 and the standard subject at 9 %, while the rack reaches only
  **9.3–9.6 %**. Relaxing the eye-height band to 0.9–2.2 m did not help. Even with no
  rack lower bound, no station met all the other constraints. The rack (1.0 × 1.1 m)
  sits right beside the hologram on the same wall. Getting it to 10 % brings the camera
  close enough that the hologram leaves the left band or the eye leaves the room
  in flight. The uncommitted tree keeps the previous attempt's station. Its 3 failing
  cases (`[library-framing]`, the Library station case and the flight room bounds)
  show this. A spec decision is needed: lower the rack floor to about 9 %, move the
  wall projector or rack, or widen the x band.

  unchanged `kRigLift`. Neither the lift nor the room limit changed. The flight

  pushed it past y = 3 m.
