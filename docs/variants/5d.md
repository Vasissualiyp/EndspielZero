# 5d - chess with a turn axis and a timeline axis

Definition: [`variants/5d.toml`](../../variants/5d.toml)

An 8×8 board extended with a **turn** axis (three boards deep) and a **timeline** axis
(two timelines wide), both declared with their axis kinds. Nothing in the engine treats
them specially: a rook is still `[1]^inf`, so here it slides along a file, a rank,
*through time*, and *between timelines*; a knight still leaps `{1,2}` on two axes, which
is how it jumps over a missing board. Only the starting position's first board is
occupied, so time travel and timeline travel are available from move one.

**Depth-1 count:** 84. Covered by the shipped-variants golden test, which also pins the
FEN-N round-trip and the oracle agreement.

**Status.** This is the *movement* half of 5D chess: turn and timeline are ordinary
lattice axes, exactly as the architecture intends (ADR-0007). The **multiverse
bookkeeping** is not enforced by the game yet. In particular a move does not append a
new board, time travel does not branch a timeline, and a turn is not yet a set of moves
on the present boards. The state model for that - playable boards, the present column,
timeline activity, and the branch-on-time-travel policy - is implemented and tested in
`src/temporal/` (`TimelineModel`, `Multiverse`); wiring it into `Game` is the remaining
M6 work. Until then this variant plays as one four-dimensional board.

**Rules.** EndspielZero's own, stated in the variant file. The board is deliberately small -
384 cells - to keep the multiverse's perft depths meaningful once they exist.
